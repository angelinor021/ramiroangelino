About

I'm a doctoral student in Counseling Psychology at Arizona State University, mentored by Dr. Oswaldo Moreno. My work centers on understanding how violence and adversity shape mental health, and on the relationships, communities, and programs that help people heal and thrive.

My research training has taken me through more than 30 federally and privately funded projects in violence prevention and mental health. Along the way, I've worked on studies with adults, adolescents, and children, including families connected to child welfare, juvenile justice, and immigration systems. A common thread across this work has been making sure evidence-based approaches truly fit the cultures and communities they are meant to serve.

As a scientist-practitioner in training, I hope to build a career where research and practice inform one another. I'm especially drawn to work that deepens our understanding of violence and mental health, strengthens prevention and intervention, and carries psychological science into practice and policy.

I welcome conversations with people across all fields and backgrounds. Some of the best ideas grow from unexpected connections, and I'd be glad to hear from you.

Research

Interpersonal violence and trauma

Understanding sexual and interpersonal violence across the lifespan, from childhood exposure to adult victimization and perpetration, to inform prevention and support for survivors.

  • Sexual violence prevention
  • Intimate partner violence
  • Aggression

Health disparities and minority stress

How discrimination, stigma, and immigration-related stress affect mental health, and how family acceptance and community support can buffer those effects.

  • LGBTQ+ mental health
  • Immigration-related stress
  • Family acceptance

Translational and implementation science

Partnering with communities and service systems to move research into practice, with attention to how evidence-based approaches are adapted across cultural and community contexts.

  • Implementation science
  • Community-engaged methods
  • Qualitative analysis
